## 1. Executive Summary
Founders waste $5,000–$15,000 and 6+ weeks on agencies or pay perpetual monthly taxes to Figma, Webflow, Squarespace, and Pingdom just to launch a brand and website.
The **Techstarz101 Brand & Web Launch** suite provides **8 standalone, click-to-launch open-source tool repos** across 2 composable stacks plus an instant Brand Kit & live HTML landing page compiler:
- Visual design systems (Penpot), headless & visual CMS (Directus / WordPress / Ghost / Strapi), self-hosted Git & CI (Gitea), automated SSL routing (Nginx Proxy Manager), and 24/7 SSL/uptime monitoring (Uptime Kuma).
