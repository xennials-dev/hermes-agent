## 1. Executive Summary & Problem Statement

Growth teams spend $1,500–$4,000/month across HubSpot Marketing Hub, Optimizely/VWO, Typeform, Bitly Enterprise, and Looker just to run A/B campaigns and attribute ad spend.
The **Techstarz101 Marketing Campaigns** suite replaces that entire rented stack with **8 standalone, click-to-launch open-source app repos** across 2 composable stacks:
- Multi-angle ad generation (Pain, Speed/Guarantee, Sovereign ROI Math, Social Proof) with automated UTM-tagged Variant A/B links.
- Bayesian A/B winner selection (GrowthBook) and real-time conversion tracking (Umami / Matomo).
- Zero-contact-tax drip sequences (Mautic / Listmonk) and interactive conversational ad funnels (Typebot).
- Executive ROAS & CAC dashboards (Metabase).
