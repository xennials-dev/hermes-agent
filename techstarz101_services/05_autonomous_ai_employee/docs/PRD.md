## 1. Executive Summary
Executives and small teams lose 15+ hours/week to repetitive operational coordination — summarizing threads, checking CRM/invoices, updating SOPs, and routing approvals.

- Persistent company vector memory (Qdrant / AnythingLLM)
- Durable multi-day workflow execution (Temporal / Dify)
- Unified multi-model routing & spend caps (Open WebUI / LiteLLM)
- Full audit observability & human-in-the-loop approval governance (Langfuse / NocoDB)
