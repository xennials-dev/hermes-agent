# Review Call & Upsell Qualification

## Trigger Conditions
Use this skill when:
- Conducting the 30-minute review call with a prospect/client
- Presenting the Action Report findings and ROI calculation
- Qualifying the client for Implementation ($3K–$10K) or AI Concierge Retainers ($1.2K–$2K/mo)

---

## 1. Review Call Protocol (30 Minutes)

1. **Pre-Call Check**:
   - Send the PDF/Markdown report 1 hour in advance.
   - Screen-share the report immediately upon starting the call.

2. **Walkthrough Agenda**:
   - Reiterate their core pain points: *"You told us X was taking 6 hours a week..."*
   - Reveal the Quick Wins & financial impact: *"Implementing these 3 tools gives you back 8 hours a week and ~$2,400/month net ROI."*
   - Present the 4-Day Quick Start plan.

3. **The Three Critical Closing Questions**:
   1. *"Of the recommendations we reviewed today, which one is the most urgent for you to solve?"*
   2. *"Do you want your internal team to execute this 4-day plan, or would you like us to handle the full build and integration for you?"*
   3. *"What is your timeline? Is this an urgent priority this month, or something you can push off for 60 days?"*
