# The 6-Step Niche Validation Framework

Determine whether a niche or product idea has genuine commercial demand, recurring willingness to pay, and long-term sustainability before investing engineering resources.

---

## 1. The 6 Systematic Validation Steps

```
[Step 1: Check Actual Demand] ──► Google Trends, Reddit search volume, People Also Ask
            │
[Step 2: Analyze Competition + Gaps] ──► Find underserved long-tail angles & local/modern twists
            │
[Step 3: Trends & Seasonality] ──► Verify 5-year steady interest (avoid short spikes)
            │
[Step 4: Profit Potential] ──► Identify high-LTV recurring purchases and B2B spending power
            │
[Step 5: Founder Energy Fit] ──► Confirm ability to produce 10+ content assets without fatigue
            │
[Step 6: Rapid Smoke Test] ──► Launch a 1-page freebie/waitlist to measure conversion rate
```

---

## 2. Validation Metrics & Scoring Rubric

| Criterion | High Value (Green) | Moderate (Yellow) | Red Flag (Do Not Launch) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Search Intent** | Commercial / "Best X for Y" | General informational | Zero active search queries |
| **Buyer Willingness** | B2B / High-income professionals | Enthusiasts / Hobbyists | Price-sensitive freebie seekers |
| **Competition Level** | Fragmented boutique providers | Moderate competitors | Giant dominant monopolies |
| **Purchase Frequency** | Monthly recurring / Retainer | Annual renewal | One-off low ticket (<$20) |
