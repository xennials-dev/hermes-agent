# Client Acquisition: 7 Zero-Cost Lead Strategies

## Trigger Conditions
Use this skill when:
- Seeking new business clients for AI Audits & Assessments
- Designing lead generation workflows, cold outreach, or partnership campaigns
- Filling the consulting pipeline without paid advertising

---

## 1. The 7 Proven Client Acquisition Channels

### Channel 1: Free Co-Working AI Meetups
- Partner with local co-working spaces (free venue for them, content for you).
- Deliver a tactical 20-minute presentation on automating routine operations.
- Offer attendees a complimentary 45-minute AI Opportunity Audit.

### Channel 2: Local Business Direct Outreach (In-Person / Door Knocking)
- Walk into 25–30 local service businesses (trades, real estate, professional services).
- Ask for the owner or operations manager.
- Offer to review one friction-heavy workflow for free. Convert 20% to paid engagements.

### Channel 3: Targeted Warm LinkedIn DMs
- Connect with local business owners (2–20 employees).
- Never pitch on message #1. Ask curiosity-driven operational questions:
  *"Hey [Name], noticing lots of teams in [Industry] getting crushed by manual quote follow-ups. How are you handling that lately?"*
- Follow up with a 90-second personalized Loom video.

### Channel 4: Network & Referral Audits
- Reach out to friends, family, gym contacts, and former colleagues who run businesses.
- *"I'm launching a new AI audit service and doing 5 free diagnostic sessions this week. Would you like a 15-minute quick look at your workflows?"*

### Channel 5: Agency & Professional Strategic Partnerships
- Partner with CPAs, bookkeepers, commercial insurance agents, and marketing agencies.
- Agree on a 10%–15% referral fee or reciprocated referrals.

### Channel 6: Weekly Co-Working "AI Office Hours"
- Host 1 hour of free walk-in troubleshooting at a regional tech hub or incubator.
- Establish authority and capture warm high-intent leads.

### Channel 7: Build in Public & Case Study Documentation
- Document before/after case studies (e.g. *"How we eliminated 8 hours of quote processing for a plumbing contractor"*).
- Share results on LinkedIn, X, and email newsletters.
