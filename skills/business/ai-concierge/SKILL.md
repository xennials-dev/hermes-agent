# AI Concierge Retainer Playbook

## Trigger Conditions
Use this skill when:
- Pitching or managing a monthly recurring AI Concierge retainer
- Structuring bi-weekly 45-minute working sessions
- Managing high-leverage client relationships ($1,000+/hr effective yield)

---

## 1. Offer Economics & Structure

- **Price**: $1,200 – $2,000 / month (Price ladder: $1,200 → $1,500 → $1,800 → $2,000)
- **Time Commitment**: Exactly two 45-minute working sessions per month (1.5 hours total)
- **Asynchronous Support**: Unlimited Voxer / Slack voice notes with guaranteed 12-hour business reply SLA
- **Effective Hourly Rate**: **$1,000+ / hour** ($1,500 / 1.5 hrs = $1,000/hr)

---

## 2. Session Delivery Blueprint

### Call 1: Foundation & High-Priority Quick Win
1. Onboard client into their dedicated Notion/Portal hub.
2. Establish their **90-Day Win Definition**: *"90 days from now, what single outcome makes this engagement an undeniable victory?"*
3. Target achieving that 90-day win within **60 days**.
4. Set up first high-impact tool or custom prompt/skill.

### Calls 2+: Bi-Weekly AOA Sprints
For each active business workflow:
1. **Audit**: Client shares screen and walks through today's workflow.
2. **Optimize**: Trim steps, cut friction in real-time.
3. **Automate**: Build or configure the Hermes/AI skill together.

---

## 3. Retainer Retention Mechanics
1. **Notion Client Hub**: Maintain a living inventory of all reclaimed hours, deployed tools, and active automations.
2. **Post-Session Executive Summary**: Send a 3-bullet recap immediately after every call.
3. **Monthly ROI Review**: Remind the client of cumulative hours saved vs. the subscription cost.
