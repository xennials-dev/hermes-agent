# AI Concierge (Monthly Retainer Service)

## Trigger Conditions
Use this skill when the user says "concierge service", "monthly retainer",
"coaching program", or when onboarding and delivering ongoing advisory to clients.

## Instructions

### The Offer Structure

- **Price Point**: $1,200–$2,000 / month
- **Deliverables**:
  - Two 45-minute strategic & implementation calls per month.
  - Unlimited asynchronous communication (Voxer / Slack / Email with 12-hour response).
  - Hands-on AI skill authoring, workflow optimization, and team upskilling.
- **Effective Hourly Rate**: $1,000+/hour ($1,500 for 1.5 total call hours).

---

### Call Cadence & Structure

#### Call 1: Foundation & Tooling Setup
- Guided onboarding and tool integrations.
- Establish context repositories, global prompts, and security policies.
- Define 90-day core objective: *"90 days from now, what makes this engagement feel like a massive win?"*

#### Calls 2+: AOA Implementation Sprints
For each active client workflow:
1. **Audit**: Review the live workflow and friction points.
2. **Optimize**: Trim extraneous steps (e.g. 14 steps down to 6).
3. **Automate**: Build reusable custom prompts, skills, or scripts.

---

### Retention & Operations Best Practices
1. **Central Notion / Portal Hub**: Keep a single live dashboard documenting all delivered tools, automations, and time saved.
2. **Post-Call Summaries**: Send immediate action items and Loom/recording links.
3. **Scarcity & Pricing**:
   - Cap active roster (e.g., 5 total clients).
   - Step pricing: $1,200 $\rightarrow$ $1,500 $\rightarrow$ $1,800 $\rightarrow$ $2,000/mo.
