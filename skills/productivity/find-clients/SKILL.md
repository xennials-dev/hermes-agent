# Client Acquisition: 7 Zero‑Cost Methods

## Trigger Conditions
Use this skill when the user says "find clients", "get customers",
"lead generation", "outreach strategy", or asks how to sell AI audits.

---

## The 7 Proven Acquisition Channels

### 1. Host an AI Meetup
- Partner with a local co-working space (free venue / co-marketing).
- Give a 20-minute live demonstration of practical AI productivity.
- Collect emails/contact info at the door.
- Follow up within 24 hours offering the 45-minute AI assessment.

### 2. Direct Door-to-Door Local Outreach
- Walk into 20–30 local businesses in a single strip/district.
- Ask to speak with the owner/general manager.
- Focus on curiosity and operational friction, not a hard pitch.

### 3. LinkedIn & Social Direct Messages
- Search local small business owners and operators.
- Start conversation around their experience with AI tools.
- Send personalized Loom videos or voice notes addressing specific pain points.

### 4. Warm Network AI Audits
- Message contacts (friends, family, past colleagues, local service providers).
- *"I'd love to spend 15 minutes showing you one high-impact way AI can save you 5 hours a week."*
- Convert warm demos into full assessments.

### 5. Strategic Agency Partnerships
- Connect with accountants, marketing agencies, insurance brokers, and IT shops.
- Offer referral commissions or reciprocal client recommendations.

### 6. Weekly AI Office Hours
- Sit at a local co-working hub or business incubator for 1–2 hours weekly.
- Offer free 15-minute AI troubleshooting to members.

### 7. Build in Public & Case Study Sharing
- Document daily implementations, client before/after metrics, and time-saving workflows on LinkedIn / X.
