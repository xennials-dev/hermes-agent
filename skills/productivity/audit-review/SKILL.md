# Review Call & Upsell Qualification

## Trigger Conditions
Use this skill when the user says "review call", "client presentation",
or after the report has been sent to the client.

## Instructions

### Phase 4: The Review Call

1. **Before the call** – Email the report to the client for prior review.

2. **During the call (30 minutes)**:
   - Screen‑share the report
   - Walk through each recommendation: "You have pain point A → here is tool B"

3. **The Three Closing Questions**:
   - "Of these recommendations, which is the most urgent for you?"
   - "Do you want to do this yourself, or would you like my help implementing?"
   - "What's your timeline? Are these pain points urgent, or can you live with them for 60 more days?"

4. **Upsell Qualification Matrix**:

   | Upsell Service | Price Range | When to Pitch |
   |----------------|-------------|---------------|
   | Process Redesign | $3K‑$3.5K | When they have broken, multi‑step processes |
   | Simple Automation | $1.5K+ | When they need Zapier/Make.com workflows |
   | Knowledge System | $3K‑$5K | When they answer repetitive questions |
   | Custom Workflows | $3K‑$8K | When they have proprietary logic |
   | AI Concierge | $1.2K‑$2K/mo | When they want ongoing coaching |

### Upsell Script Example

> "That's great. We've identified several quick wins you can implement yourself.
> But I noticed a few larger opportunities in the 'Major Projects' quadrant.
> These would save you even more time, but they require a bit more setup.
> Would you like me to help you implement those as well?"

### Pro Tip
Offer to credit the assessment fee toward implementation:
> "How about I credit the $999 you already paid toward the implementation?"
