## 3. Recommended Quick-Win Solutions

### Solution 1: Fathom / Fireflies.ai
- **Primary Benefit:** Automatically records, transcribes, and summarizes client meetings with action items synced to CRM.
- **Monthly Cost:** $19/month
- **Setup Time:** ~10 minutes
- **Estimated Time Reclaimed:** **4.0 hrs/week**
- **Implementation Category:** Meeting Intelligence

### Solution 2: Custom GPT / Claude Knowledge Hub
- **Primary Benefit:** Instantly answers repetitive client inquiries from uploaded documents and drafts email responses.
- **Monthly Cost:** $20/month
- **Setup Time:** ~30 minutes
- **Estimated Time Reclaimed:** **8.0 hrs/week**
- **Implementation Category:** Customer & Team Knowledge Base

### Solution 3: Dext / QuickBooks AI Invoicing
- **Primary Benefit:** Automates receipt capture, expense categorization, and recurring client invoice generation.
- **Monthly Cost:** $25/month
- **Setup Time:** ~20 minutes
- **Estimated Time Reclaimed:** **3.5 hrs/week**
- **Implementation Category:** Financial Automation

### Solution 4: Fillout AI + Cal.com
- **Primary Benefit:** Dynamic smart forms that qualify leads and book directly into your calendar without back-and-forth emails.
- **Monthly Cost:** $15/month
- **Setup Time:** ~15 minutes
- **Estimated Time Reclaimed:** **3.0 hrs/week**
- **Implementation Category:** Lead Intake & Scheduling

### Solution 5: Make.com / Zapier Smart Workflows
- **Primary Benefit:** Connects existing tools so data flows automatically without manual copy-pasting between spreadsheets.
- **Monthly Cost:** $29/month
- **Setup Time:** ~45 minutes
- **Estimated Time Reclaimed:** **6.0 hrs/week**
- **Implementation Category:** Process Automation
